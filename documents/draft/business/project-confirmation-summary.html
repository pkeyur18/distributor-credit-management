The short version

What we're building

You run a referral network — every member introduced by an existing one — holding 500 to 5,000 people. Each month you record how active each member was, and work out what everyone has earned, based on their own activity and everyone beneath them. Today that's done by hand. We're building a private system, for your use only, that holds the whole structure, takes your monthly figures, works out every reward the instant you save, closes the books safely each month, and hands you the reports you need.

Why it's worth building

Today, vs. after launch

Today — by hand

  • Hours of level-by-level working; one slip near the bottom corrupts everything above it
  • The structure lives partly on paper, partly in memory
  • A member's question means rebuilding the answer from scratch
  • Once a month passes, nothing reliable is kept of it
  • A yearly view means stitching twelve months together by hand

After — the system

  • Every reward correct the instant one figure is saved
  • One place holds the whole structure, permanently
  • Any member's full breakdown is one search away
  • Every month is locked into a permanent record before it's cleared
  • Yearly averages, produced as a spreadsheet in a few clicks
Five things, in order

What the system does

01

Holds the structure

Every member, their number, and who introduced them — fixed permanently from the moment they join.

02

Takes your figures

Find a member, enter their Business Volume, done. One field. No currency, no mode to choose.

03

Calculates instantly

The moment you save, every affected reward up to the top of the structure is correct.

04

Closes each month safely

Takes a backup, writes a permanent record, only then clears the month's live figures.

05

Produces your reports

Monthly figures, yearly averages, and a list of who's fallen quiet — as spreadsheets.

Step by step

How a month works

1

Activity is recorded, all month

You search for a member and record their figure whenever activity happens. Takes seconds.

2

Everything recalculates on the spot

No "recalculate" button anywhere — every affected figure is already correct on screen.

3

The month ends

An alert appears on every screen naming the month, and new recording pauses until it's closed.

4

You close it, on your schedule

A backup is taken and confirmed first — if it fails, nothing is touched. Only then is a permanent record written for every member.

5

The month clears, reporting stays

Live figures reset to zero for the new month; the closed month's record and reports remain, forever.
